# Fiverr gig video: "Motion Graphics That Bring Ideas to Life" (by Evan)

16:9, 1920x1080, 60 fps, **32 s**. A silent tempo of 96 bpm (one beat = 0.625 s, one bar = 2.5 s); every key pose
lands on it. The sound was designed first: `audio/sfx_cues.json` (made by `audio/make_cues.py`) is the timing
source of truth, and the animation is built to it. Cue sheet: `audio/sfx_cuesheet.md`.

Look (from the thumbnail): deep green-black ground, a terracotta centre column, cream serif display type
(roman + italic), small tracked sans-serif labels, technical chrome (ruler ticks, timecodes, REC waveform,
crosshairs, dashed circles, a bezier curve, concentric rings, three leaning ellipses, a play button).

Sound palette: warm, tactile, editorial. Felt thuds, wood/letterpress knocks, pencil draw-ons, a mechanical
button, dark sweeps, film shutters and timeline snaps, over a low D drone and a soft pulse on each bar.
**No pop sounds** (client preference); nothing bright or chirpy.
